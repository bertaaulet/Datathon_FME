## Crux
Design a dashboard that can help you take decisions about the main challenge in which your team is participating.
- Select relevant information and show what conclusions it yields and what decisions it helps to substantiate.
- You may choose whatever format you think is most suitable to communicate your results. Power BI, Shiny, Tableau, a web app…

The judges will base themselves on the following criteria in order to evaluate your dashboard.
- Usefulness to decision-making _Does your dashboard help to answer the questions that your challenge poses Does it provide a useful basis for decisions_
- Adequateness _Are the representations in your dashboard appropriate for the data and the comparisons_
- Coherence _Do the indicators, charts, and conclusions align with one another Do they align with the data_

There will be three finalists, only one of which will win the prize.
